Frequently Asked Questions & Quick Actions

How is my Career Readiness Score calculated? ▼
Your score is computed in real time using 4 main weighted components: Resume Quality (40%), Job Requirement Skill Coverage (35%), Active Skill Density (15%), and Mock Interview Performance (10%).
How do I earn a Course Completion Certificate? ▼
Go to the Courses tab, view recommended learning modules, and complete the coursework. Once marked completed, a verified Certificate with a unique verification code will automatically be generated in your Certifications tab.
Can I update my resume anytime? ▼
Yes! Click "+ Update resume" at the top right of the dashboard. You can re-upload an updated PDF/DOCX or manually adjust your skills and experience anytime.
Are placement drive recommendations real? ▼
Placement drive profiles match campus hiring benchmarks. Eligibility is computed directly from your uploaded profile skills and academic criteria.
How do I add my Certificate to my LinkedIn Profile? ▼
Click "View Certificate" on any completed course badge, then click "Share Verified Badge" or "Add to LinkedIn". Paste the pre-formatted credential ID and URL into your LinkedIn Licenses & Certifications section.
